# 1. Have a note taking place
It's important to have a place to dump your thoughts.
I am not talking about those yt people "My favourite Obsidian Theme + Setup for optimal second brain zettelkansensomething"

i mean a place where you visit and just write something and go on about your day

The best skill i ever learned is to write while thinking,

which reminds me of a great quote:
"your mind is made for thinking not for holding thoughts"

Personally i like using `usememos/memos` its oss, google keep alternative. on the physical side i have this small form factor diary

# 2. Start thinking from the basics

clear thinking means you can think straight through a dense, opaque problem on which you couldn't even think about a starting point.

examples of problems like these are "why is my company not making money" which is a question i faced.

at times like this, what are you supposed to think about first?

my approach is to just start from the basics
instead ask yourself "what does it mean by a company to make money" is it "making products", "working faster" etc.

then try to work your way upwards until you are left with some actionable tasks that contribute to the question
